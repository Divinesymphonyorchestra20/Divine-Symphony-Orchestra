## 1. Executive summary
Plan, design and develop an official website presenting DSO as an orchestral institution with a distinct musical identity and clearly communicated relationship with Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries (MFM). The site should communicate history, people, music, performances, ministry context, achievements and continuing work. Audiences include members, MFM members, concert audiences, musicians, collaborators, event organisers, partners, researchers and people interested in DSO history.

The first phase is planning and documentation. Visual design and implementation follow in later phases. The website structure should reserve replaceable spaces for photographs, recordings, posters and other media.

**Core principle:** Build around DSO's real identity and verified history. Design should support the institution, not invent a new identity or replace its story.

## 2. Project background and identity
Biography One describes DSO as a Nigerian orchestral ensemble based in Lagos and associated with the music ministry of MFM. It developed from a small string ensemble into a larger orchestral body with a wider range of instrumental families.

The supplied Instagram profile identifies the account as @divine.symphonyorchestra and uses the phrase “Excellence. Precision. Spiritual artistry.” These identity cues require leadership confirmation. Keep the DSO logo as the primary identity mark. The MFM emblem is a separate ministry mark and must not be merged into the DSO logo without permission.

Recommended positioning: a ministry-associated orchestra with a strong symphonic identity. Do not present DSO as a generic church website, conceal its MFM relationship, or imply independent commercial status unless leadership confirms that positioning.

## 3. Project objectives
1. Establish an authoritative online home for DSO.
2. Present identity, history, mission, values and MFM relationship.
3. Preserve institutional memory through founder, former leadership and alumni recognition.
4. Introduce current leadership, musicians, instrumental sections and membership information.
5. Promote concerts, events, repertoire, performances and recordings.
6. Provide an organised media and gallery area.
7. Communicate ministry service, education, outreach and impact where verified.
8. Publish news, announcements, stories and updates.
9. Provide routes for enquiries, invitations, collaborations and audition or membership enquiries, subject to approved processes.
10. Create a maintainable content structure that can grow as records and media are supplied.

## 4. Audiences and needs
- MFM members: understand DSO's role, find performances and approved media.
- General public and concert audiences: discover DSO, music, events and attendance information.
- Current members: approved member information or secure resources if later authorised.
- Prospective musicians: understand sections and approved audition/joining processes.
- Former members and alumni: find the legacy archive and contribute memories.
- Founders and former leaders: accurate recognition and a way to provide recollections or corrections.
- Event organisers and collaborators: understand DSO's capabilities and submit enquiries.
- Researchers, media and institutions: access verified history, milestones, biographies, repertoire and press material.

## 5. Website architecture: 13 principal pages
1. **Home** — introduce DSO and direct visitors to key content.
2. **About DSO** — identity, purpose, mission, vision, values and organisation.
3. **Our Heritage** — chronology, milestones and historical development.
4. **Our Story** — accessible narrative of the Orchestra.
5. **Our Legacy** — founders, former leaders, past contributors and their influence.
6. **The Orchestra** — current artistic leadership, conductors, sections and ensemble structure.
7. **Meet the Members** — member profiles and, if approved, member-focused resources.
8. **Music & Performances** — repertoire, recordings and performance themes.
9. **Concerts & Events** — upcoming and past events and attendance information.
10. **Media & Gallery** — photographs, video, audio, posters and press materials.
11. **Ministry & Impact** — ministry role, service, education, outreach and community contribution.
12. **News & Stories** — announcements, interviews, features, achievements and updates.
13. **Contact** — official contact routes and collaboration enquiries.

## 6. Page-by-page content requirements
### Home
Prominent logo and navigation; replaceable performance hero; concise introduction; featured event or fallback; highlights to Legacy, Orchestra, Music and Ministry; selected media; latest stories; calls to action; footer with approved contacts, social links, MFM wording and legal/privacy links.

### About DSO
Official description and organisational context; approved mission, vision and values; authorised MFM relationship wording; overview of development and current work; links to Heritage, Story and Legacy.

### Our Heritage
Chronological timeline of founding, growth, instrumental expansion, performances, training and milestones; historical material with verified captions/dates; milestone detail pages where useful; source notes and editorial review. Unconfirmed claims remain internal pending verification.

### Our Story
Narrative explaining how DSO began, developed and serves its musical and ministry purpose; chapters supported by verified biography material and first-hand accounts; selected photographs, quotations, archives and video; distinguish documented facts from recollections.

### Our Legacy
Dedicated recognition of the seven founders; individual profiles where information and consent are available; archive of former conductors, directors, music directors, section leaders and other significant roles with confirmed titles and service periods; alumni recollections/interviews; legacy timeline; correction and consent process.

### The Orchestra
Approved current leadership and conducting structure; confirmed instrumental sections; artistic, musical and administrative roles; guest artists and collaborators where relevant; links to profiles, events and repertoire.

### Meet the Members
Member directory and profiles with approved names, portraits, instruments, sections, roles and biographies; filters if useful; member-only content requires access controls. Do not publish private contact details or images without permission.

### Music & Performances
Musical identity and verified repertoire; composer, title, arrangement, date and programme context where available; categories may include Western classical, African/Nigerian, Christian and other approved works; recordings only with rights cleared.

### Concerts & Events
Upcoming events with title, date, time, venue, description, poster and attendance/booking details; past archive with programmes, performers and media; clear status labels. Do not add ticketing/booking until the process and responsible contact are confirmed.

### Media & Gallery
Organised photographs, video, audio, posters, programmes and press coverage; albums by event, year, section or subject; captions, dates, credits and usage permissions; replaceable placeholders and accessible alt text.

### Ministry & Impact
Explain ministry service and MFM context; document education, training, outreach and community work only when verified; participant stories require consent and factual support.

### News & Stories
Announcements, interviews, member features, event reports, achievements and updates; categories, dates and editor attribution; editorial draft/review/approval workflow.

### Contact
Approved official email, phone, venue or postal details; enquiry categories; secure form, spam protection and privacy notice; official social links. Never invent contact information.

## 7. Editorial distinctions
- **Our Heritage:** chronology and records — what happened, and when?
- **Our Story:** institutional narrative — how did DSO develop, and what does it stand for?
- **Our Legacy:** people and enduring contributions — who built, led and shaped DSO?
- **Meet the Members:** current people — who is part of the Orchestra now?

These pages should link to one another but should not be collapsed into one page.

## 8. Historical foundation and research notes
Biography One records DSO's development from a seven-player string ensemble into a larger orchestra including woodwind and brass; the former name D.K.O. Symphony Orchestra / D.K.O. Orchestra; repertoire spanning Western classical, African, Nigerian and Christian compositions; Symphonies of Praise III in May 2016 marking the ninth anniversary; and participation by selected musicians in the Stellenbosch International Chamber Music Festival, including 2016. It attributes the original leadership/founding vision to Dele Olutade, but role and chronology require confirmation. A complete leadership history remains to be assembled.

**Founding date:** 6 March 2007 is the confirmed date and should be used consistently. The seven founders' names, roles and personal histories remain outstanding.

Other items requiring confirmation include the official founding story; all founder identities; name-change chronology; dates of ensemble expansion; instrumental section formation; former conductors and leaders; concert and anniversary records; international festival participation; repertoire catalogue; and current leadership/membership structure.

The bibliography in Biography One includes academic, institutional and media sources. Claims should be checked against the original publications before public use; secondary profile sites require cross-checking.

## 9. Design direction
Recommended creative direction: classical elegance with a contemporary, cinematic presentation; refined and expressive, while remaining readable and easy to navigate. Use the existing DSO logo; use approved performance imagery; combine editorial typography with accessible body text; keep motion restrained; maintain contrast and responsive layouts; do not imply an unapproved identity change.

Suggested palette is provisional: charcoal/near-black, ivory/warm white, champagne/muted gold, possible royal purple, and original logo colours sampled from high-resolution artwork. This is not an approved brand standard.

Logo handling: preserve DSO lettering, musical staff lines, upper magenta/yellow forms and lower multicoloured motif; create variants only as approved adaptations; keep MFM emblem separate absent an approved lock-up; do not use AI-generated logo approximations.

## 10. Media and content placeholders
Reserve replaceable spaces for: homepage hero; orchestra group image; founder group and portraits; former leaders; current members; conductors/leadership; concert posters and event media; gallery; history archive; impact stories. Use clearly labelled placeholders. Do not present stock or AI-generated imagery as actual DSO members, events or history.

## 11. Functional and technical requirements
Responsive navigation/layouts; SEO titles, descriptions, headings and URLs; accessibility including contrast, keyboard navigation, labels and alt text; news/event management; member/leadership profile management; media library; secure contact forms; social links and approved embeds; privacy-conscious analytics; backups, security updates and role-based administration.

Member area is undecided: public directory, private portal or both. A secure portal requires a confirmed need, permitted users, data scope, authentication and administration.

Provisional technology options only: Next.js/React; Tailwind or equivalent; optional restrained motion library; Sanity or WordPress CMS; managed media storage; Vercel or another host; secure form provider; privacy-conscious analytics. These are not final commitments.

## 12. Content management and data structure
Reusable content types: Person/Member; Founder profile; Former leadership record; Event; News/Story; Media item; Repertoire item; Milestone; Page content. Separate public information from private/internal information and avoid collecting sensitive personal details without an approved operational need.

## 13. Governance, accuracy, consent and editorial standards
Assign an authorised DSO reviewer; obtain permission for identifiable photographs, stories, interviews and contact details; confirm media rights; attribute oral histories; mark claims requiring verification; distinguish primary records, published research, secondary profiles and recollections; provide corrections process; use privacy notices; obtain MFM approval for official descriptions/marks/relationship wording where required.

## 14. Implementation roadmap
1. **Phase 1 – Planning foundation:** agree identity, objectives, sitemap, requirements, historical basis, media plan, technical options and governance. Gate: plan reviewed and approved.
2. **Phase 2 – Visual design:** design system, navigation, homepage and representative inner pages; test desktop/mobile. Gate: approved visual direction and page designs.
3. **Phase 3 – Website shell and page development:** templates, navigation, responsive layouts and reusable components. Gate: functional structure with placeholders.
4. **Phase 4 – Database and content management:** content types, editor roles, media, forms and any approved member access. Gate: working content administration.
5. **Phase 5 – Content and media population:** approved biographies, people, events, stories, history and media. Gate: content-complete review version.
6. **Phase 6 – Testing and quality assurance:** responsive, accessibility, performance, forms, links, security, content and browser tests. Gate: critical issues resolved and launch approval.
7. **Phase 7 – Deployment and maintenance:** publish, configure domain/analytics/backups and assign update responsibilities. Gate: live site and maintenance process.
