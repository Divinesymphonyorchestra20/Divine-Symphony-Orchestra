THE ORCHESTRA

Music with
meaning.

Divine Symphony Orchestra is a Nigerian orchestral ensemble associated with the music ministry of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries.

From its beginnings as a seven-player string ensemble, DSO has developed into a broader orchestral body. Its musical work spans Western classical repertoire alongside African, Nigerian and Christian compositions.

OUR STORY · SINCE 2007

Rooted in purpose.
Growing through music.

Founded on 6 March 2007, the Orchestra began with seven people and a shared commitment to musical service. Its story continues through the musicians, leaders and supporters who have contributed along the way.
